# KEMSOR Studios — current design direction

The deliverable is a seven-page React website using Vite: Home, About, Services, Projects, News, Careers and Contact. The new supporting pages extend the existing identity; removed template sample pages stay removed.

## Visual direction

- Image-led cinematic opening with a concise display headline and direct project CTA.
- Supplied gold emblem, Cinema Black (#090909), Studio Charcoal (#181614), Kemsor Gold (#D9A441), Gold Highlight (#F5D890), Deep Bronze (#85511E), and Warm Ivory (#F4EFE5). Shared tokens and component rules live in src/brand.css.
- Bebas Neue for major display headings and the KEMSOR video letters; Manrope for service names and body text.
- Six edge-to-edge expanding panels with silent footage, directly beneath the opening.
- Varied content hierarchy: editorial introduction, four format stills, six cinematic service cards that stack while scrolling, a six-step accordion, and a gold closing invitation.
- Shared page navigation, homepage section anchors, mobile navigation, keyboard interactions and reduced-motion support.

## Content and boundaries

The two supplied Word documents provide the content and service structure. The latest user request restores their multipage outline. The homepage retains its original media and scroll experiences; supporting pages use readable editorial layouts.

The footage is a visual exploration rather than credited studio work. Contact currently provides a downloadable project brief, clearly identified as a local download. Live enquiry delivery, CMS, analytics, contact details and deployment are outside the current completed implementation.

The services section follows the supplied stacked-image reference: subtly squared image cards pin near the top as successive cards scroll over them. Six generated scenes depict the production office, location planning, casting, shooting, set design and color grading. Each card carries the exact service capability names from the supplied document.

## Client identity handoff

The Gold on Black identity extends the logo’s camera lens, film strip and diamond details into a consistent digital system. Use warm gold for action and emphasis, black for cinematic space, and ivory for editorial content. The formerly orange scroll reveal now uses a warm gold field with dark text. Brand colours are UI colours; imagery retains natural colour.

Client guide: output/brand/KEMSOR-Brand-Identity.pdf (six pages), with a self-contained HTML source and KEMSOR-brand-tokens.json. Includes palette, typography, voice, logo usage, graphic motifs, motion and example applications. The supplied SVG contains raster artwork; request a true vector master for future print fabrication.
